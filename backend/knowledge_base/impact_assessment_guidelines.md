# MVP Severity Assessment Guidance: Impact Assessment Principles

> **Notice**: This document represents internal MVP Severity Assessment Guidance for decision support only. It does not represent or claim to be an official FDA, EMA, GMP, or ICH regulatory standard.

## 1. Principles of Deviation Impact Assessment
Assessing impact requires systematic evaluation of potential risks across four key dimensions:
1. **Patient Safety Impact**: Direct or indirect risk to end-user health or clinical safety.
2. **Product Quality & CQA Impact**: Risk to chemical purity, potency, sterility, dissolution, bioavailability, or degradation.
3. **Process Control & Equipment Impact**: Disruption of validated state, qualified equipment boundaries, or reproducible processing steps.
4. **Compliance & Regulatory Impact**: Discrepancies affecting batch disposition, registration dossiers, or good practice documentation.

---

## 2. Key Evidence Indicators

### Immediate Containment Status
- **Batch On Hold**: If the affected material or batch has been segregated or placed on automated MES quarantine, the immediate propagation risk is mitigated.
- **Uncontained / Distributed**: If material proceeded to downstream formulation or distribution, risk escalates toward Major or Critical.

### Magnitude and Duration of Excursion
- Evaluate whether the excursion parameter (temperature, pressure, speed, duration, pH, dissolved oxygen) exceeded proven acceptable ranges (PAR) or critical operating ranges (NOR).
- Transient excursions (e.g. seconds or brief spikes) often carry lower risk than sustained out-of-trend conditions.

### Product Contact vs Non-Product Contact
- Non-product contact equipment alarms (e.g., secondary chiller loop, exterior cleanroom differential pressure transient) typically represent Minor or Moderate risk if cleanroom containment remained uncompromised.
- Direct contact utilities (e.g., Water for Injection, clean steam, sterile compressed air) require elevated investigation (Moderate to Major).

---

## 3. Decision-Support Conflict Resolution
- When automated deterministic rules suggest a severity that differs from an AI contextual synthesis, the divergence must be explicitly documented and presented to the Quality Reviewer.
- The human Quality reviewer retains complete authority to select the final severity based on detailed investigation findings.
