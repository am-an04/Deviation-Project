# MVP Severity Assessment Guidance: Process & Operational Deviation Guidelines

> **Notice**: This document represents internal MVP Severity Assessment Guidance for decision support only. It does not represent or claim to be an official FDA, EMA, GMP, or ICH regulatory standard.

## 1. Process Deviation Categories

### 1.1 Time and Duration Deviations
- **Blending / Mixing Overrun**: Exceeding mixing durations may generate heat, degrade heat-sensitive APIs, or cause de-mixing/segregation.
  - *Decision Guidance*: If contained and placed on hold without confirmed quality loss, categorize as **Moderate**; requires blend uniformity and assay testing.
- **Hold Time Excursions**: Exceeding validated intermediate hold times presents bioburden or degradation risk.
  - *Decision Guidance*: Moderate to Major depending on bioburden controls and product sensitivity.

### 1.2 Thermal & Environmental Deviations
- **Bioreactor Temperature Drift**: Deviation during fermentation or cell culture.
  - *Decision Guidance*: Short excursions with immediate recovery are typically **Moderate**; sustained shifts altering cell viability or glycosylation profile are **Major**.
- **Cold Chain / Storage Excursions**: Controlled temperature room (+2°C to +8°C or -20°C).
  - *Decision Guidance*: If Mean Kinetic Temperature (MKT) and stability data support exposure, classify as **Minor** or **Moderate**. If exceeding stability limits, classify as **Major**.

### 1.3 Mechanical & Filtration Integrity Deviations
- **Filter Integrity Test Failures**: Post-filtration bubble point or pressure decay failure on sterilizing-grade 0.22 μm filters.
  - *Decision Guidance*: Presumed **Major** until re-testing, product bubble point ratio check, or pre-filtration bioburden verification can substantiate sterility assurance.
- **Equipment Speed & Agitation Excursions**: Agitator stoppage or speed variation during crystallization or precipitation.
  - *Decision Guidance*: Moderate; particle size distribution and crystal morphology must be verified.

### 1.4 Documentation and Labeling Deviations
- **Missing Signatures / Verifications**: Omission of secondary witness signature on batch record step.
  - *Decision Guidance*: **Minor** if procedural action can be reconstructed through audit trails, historians, or SCADA timestamps.
  - *Decision Guidance*: **Moderate** if critical process step execution cannot be unambiguously confirmed without QA investigation.
